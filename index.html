Privacy Policy
Istighfar Counter & Tracker

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Developer: Saadhat
English · العربية

Free, ad-free and offline

Istighfar Counter is completely free and ad-free. There are no premium tiers, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising SDKs or developer analytics SDKs. No account is required. Counting, themes, progress and backup/restore work offline when you choose local storage. The developer does not operate a server that receives your counting records.

Your data stays on your device

The app stores counts, phrases, daily totals, session times, goals, history and preferences in its private Android storage. Progress comparisons are calculated locally. It does not collect location, contacts, camera or microphone recordings, screen content or advertising identifiers. It does not transmit your counting history.

Pocket Mode and optional Shizuku counting

Pocket Mode keeps a fully black, dimmed screen awake. Taps can count; an optional touch lock prevents accidental touches. Android Back exits. An optional Shizuku mode handles volume-key press and release events over other apps and with the display asleep. It requires separate authorization and explicit Start. A foreground notification provides Pause, Resume and Stop. You can stop it and revoke Shizuku access.

Volume-key events are processed locally. The app does not use an Accessibility service, capture screenshots, record other apps or play silent audio. Vibration, wake-lock and foreground notification permissions support feedback and these user-controlled features.

Backup and restore

You can export an unencrypted JSON file containing counts, full session history and settings using Android's file picker. You choose the location. A cloud provider you select may store it under its own terms; the developer does not receive it. Keep backups private. Restore replaces local data after confirmation and keeps a private recovery copy until the next restore overwrites it or app storage is deleted. Android permission grants are not exported.

External pages

The app links to this privacy policy and the developer’s portfolio in your browser. External pages are governed by their own privacy policies. The app does not contain payment links, payment processing, contribution requests or contributor-only benefits. It does not collect payment details.

Retention, deletion and security

Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private counts, settings and the recovery copy. Exported files remain in your chosen storage location; delete them there if no longer wanted. The app disables its own Android automatic backup. There is no app account or developer-hosted counting database to delete. Android's sandbox protects private files; security also depends on your operating system and screen lock.

If you email us for support, we receive what you choose to include, use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations. Do not send sensitive history unless necessary.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, including questions from parents or guardians, contact Saadhat at saadhat10@gmail.com. We will update this policy and its effective date when relevant practices change.
