Privacy Policy
Istighfar Counter & Tracker

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · Developer: Saadhat
English · العربية

Istighfar Counter & Tracker is an Android app for counting dhikr and viewing your personal progress. This policy explains its local storage, advertising and optional screen-off counting. For privacy questions, contact saadhat10@gmail.com.

Your counts stay on your device

The app saves your counts, selected phrases, session times, daily goals, history and preferences in its private storage on your Android device. We do not operate an account system or a server that receives these records. The app's progress comparisons are calculated locally. We do not send your dhikr counts, selected phrases, goals or session records to Google AdMob.

Counting and reviewing saved progress work without an internet connection. Network access is used for advertising and privacy messages, and when you choose to open an external link.

Advertising and Google services

Banner advertising is disabled in the current version. The app does not load or show advertisements and does not automatically start its ad-consent request flow. It retains Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP) integration for a possible future optional advertising feature. This policy will be updated before such a feature is enabled.

The following describes Google's SDK data handling when advertising is used. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related data to serve and measure ads, provide diagnostics and prevent fraud.

According to Google's SDK disclosures, this can include your IP address and an approximate location inferred from it, advertising or other device/account identifiers, app and ad interactions, and technical performance information. The app does not request GPS location, microphone, camera, contacts or storage access for advertising. Advertising data is handled by Google and its partners under their policies and your applicable privacy choices.

See Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information for advertising, and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Your advertising choices

In a version using advertising, Google's consent message lets you make privacy choices before the app requests ads where required. When available, you can revisit these choices through Settings → Ad privacy choices. Android's privacy/advertising settings also let you manage or delete the advertising ID; names and availability vary by Android version. If consent services, ads or the network are unavailable, the counter continues to work.

Optional Shizuku screen-off counting

You may separately enable an advanced mode that uses Shizuku to process physical volume-key press and release events for counting while other apps are open or the screen is off. This requires your explicit authorization and a separate Start action. A foreground notification provides pause/resume and stop controls. You can stop the mode and revoke Shizuku access.

This feature does not use an Accessibility service, take screenshots, record screen content or play silent audio. Volume-key events are used locally to update the count and are not sent to an advertising service. Android permissions for vibration, wake locks and a foreground notification support feedback and this user-started mode.

Retention, deletion and security

Local counts and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes those local records, so save anything you need first. The app disables its own Android automatic backup. There is no app account or developer-hosted dhikr database to delete.

Android's app sandbox protects the app's private files; device security also depends on your operating system and screen-lock settings. Google states that its advertising SDK transmits collected data using TLS. Google's and its partners' retention practices are governed by their policies; uninstalling this app does not itself delete information already processed by them. Use their privacy controls or contact them for related requests.

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to include. Do not send sensitive counting history unless it is necessary for your request. We use support correspondence to respond and resolve the issue, and retain it only as needed for support or applicable obligations.

Children, updates and contact

Parents or guardians with questions about a child's use of the app may contact us. We may update this policy as the app or its services change and will update the effective date here. For questions about this policy or requests concerning information you have sent directly to us, contact Saadhat at saadhat10@gmail.com.
